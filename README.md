## About me

I'm an **economics graduate (University of Zambia)** and a **Microsoft-certified Power BI analyst** working as a title searcher in Lusaka. I like problems where the answer is in the data, and work that shouldn't be done by hand.

- 📊 I analyse data in **Power BI, Excel and SQL**, and I care about getting the numbers *right*, not just making them look good.
- 🛠️ I build practical tools with **Claude Code**. My search tool, Doxa, is used by my team at work.
- 🌍 I'm most interested in **economic and financial questions in Zambia and the region**.
